Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026
This policy is written to apply across multiple apps. Individual apps may use different device permissions or features. Where an app has additional privacy disclosures, those disclosures are presented in the app, its App Store listing, or both.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to mobile applications published under the Sultonov Apps name and to this support website. It explains how information may be handled when you use our apps or contact support.

2. Sultonov Files / FileHub-specific handling

Sultonov Files is designed as a local-first file manager. Files that you import, scan, edit, index, play, archive, or store in the Private Vault are processed on the device unless you explicitly choose a feature that communicates with another device or server.

WebDAV, SMB, and SFTP connections are made directly from your device to the server you configure. Saved connection secrets are stored using Apple Keychain APIs where the app offers credential saving. File transfer and synchronization content is not routed through a Sultonov Apps backend.

Wi-Fi Transfer starts a temporary local-network server only when you enable that feature. The transfer is intended for devices on the same trusted local network and stops when the app goes into the background. The built-in browser and Download by Link contact the websites or HTTPS addresses you choose.

On-device search may index file names, tags, PDF text, and OCR text in local application storage so that you can search your own library. Private Vault access can use Face ID, Touch ID, or the device passcode through Apple device-owner authentication.

3. Information you provide

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, the app name, device information, screenshots, and the content of your support request. We use this information only to respond to your request, investigate issues, and improve support.

4. Device permissions and user content

Depending on the features of a particular app, the app may request access to device resources such as Photos, Camera, Files, Microphone, or notifications. Access is requested only when needed for a feature and is controlled by your device settings.

User content such as photos, videos, documents, or audio may be processed on-device or, where a feature requires an online service, transmitted as necessary to provide that feature. App-specific disclosures should be reviewed before using features that upload content.

5. Purchases and subscriptions

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your full payment card details. Apple may provide transaction-related information needed to validate purchases, restore entitlements, and manage subscription access.

6. Diagnostics and analytics

Some apps may use diagnostics, crash reporting, or analytics technologies to understand reliability and performance. When used, the relevant app’s App Store privacy information and any in-app disclosures provide additional details.

7. Third-party services

Some app features may rely on Apple services or other service providers, for example for purchases, cloud processing, crash reporting, analytics, or AI-powered features. Those providers may process information according to their own privacy policies. We aim to use only the information necessary to provide the requested functionality.

8. Data retention

9. Data sharing and sale
