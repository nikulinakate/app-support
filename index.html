Purchases & Subscriptions

App Store purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. You can review or cancel active subscriptions in your Apple Account.

Restore Purchases

Open the app and look for “Restore Purchases” on the paywall or in Settings. Use the same Apple Account that made the original purchase.

Frequently asked questions

These answers apply to most of our apps. For app-specific help, email support and include the app name.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Open the App Store, tap your profile picture, choose Subscriptions, select the app, and choose Cancel Subscription. You can also use the “Manage Subscriptions” button above.

How do I restore a purchase?

Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account used for the original purchase. Then open the app and tap “Restore Purchases,” if available.

I was charged unexpectedly. What should I do?

Subscription billing is handled by Apple. Review your purchase history and subscription status in your Apple Account. For refund requests, use Apple’s official “Report a Problem” service.

The app is not working correctly.

Restart the app and your device, then install the latest app and iOS updates. If the issue continues, email support with the app name, device model, iOS version, and screenshots if relevant.

How do I report a privacy concern?

Email us at sultonovmuzafar1975@gmail.com with “Privacy Request” in the subject line.
